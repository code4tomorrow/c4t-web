:::objectives
- Put scripts on the Stage as well as on a sprite.
- Loop background music without it cutting off.
- Run several `forever` loops at once without them getting in each other's way.
- Build "do it, then undo it" moves (a jump and a pulse) from two matching `repeat` loops.
- Choose `start sound` or `play sound until done` for each job.
:::

Turn the music up. Today you're building a dance party where the music never stops, the lights flash, and you're the DJ. Arrow keys slide the dancer around, space makes it jump, up arrow makes it pulse to the beat, and clicking it makes it sing. Almost every piece uses this week's three ideas, events, loops, and sounds, all at once.

Here's who does what. Seven scripts, two of them on the Stage:

| Who | Starts with | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stage | green flag | plays music forever |
| Stage | green flag | switches backdrops every 2 seconds |
| Cat | green flag | resets, then dances in place forever |
| Cat | left and right arrows | slides left and right |
| Cat | space | jumps |
| Cat | up arrow | grows and shrinks in a quick pulse |
| Cat | a click | changes color and meows |

And here's how they play together after you click the flag. The three loops run side by side, and the key and click scripts jump in whenever you press something:

```mermaid caption="One green flag starts three loops at once; keys and clicks start the rest"
sequenceDiagram
    actor DJ as You
    participant Stage
    participant Cat
    DJ->>Stage: click the green flag
    par
        Stage->>Stage: music loop
    and
        Stage->>Stage: lights loop
    and
        Cat->>Cat: dance loop
    end
    DJ->>Cat: arrows, space, up arrow
    DJ->>Cat: click the cat
    Cat-->>DJ: meow and a new color
```

## Step 1: Set up the dance floor

Start a new project (**File > New**).

1.  Click **Choose a Backdrop** and add three party backdrops. **Party**, **Concert**, **Spotlight**, and **Neon Tunnel** all work well.
2.  Click the **Stage** button (bottom right), then open the **Backdrops** tab.
3.  Right-click the plain white `backdrop1` and choose **delete**, so a blank white screen doesn't flash up during the light show.

## Step 2: Loop the music

With the **Stage** still selected, open the **Sounds** tab. Click **Choose a Sound**, click the **Loops** category at the top, and choose a song. We used **Dance Celebrate**, but any loop works.

Go to the **Code** tab (still on the Stage) and build this:

```scratch title="On the Stage: music"
when green flag clicked
forever
    play sound (Dance Celebrate v) until done
end
```

Click the green flag. The music plays, and when the song ends it starts right over. Click the stop sign to stop it.

:::warning
Use `play sound until done` here, not `start sound`. A `forever` loop with `start sound` never waits, so it restarts the song again and again, many times a second. All you'd hear is a buzz or a stutter of the first moment of the song.
:::

Why put the music on the Stage instead of the cat? The music belongs to the whole party, not one dancer. If you delete the cat or add five more dancers later, the music keeps going.

## Step 3: Flash the lights

Still on the Stage, add a second script. Both Stage scripts start when you click the flag, and they run at the same time:

```scratch title="On the Stage: lights"
when green flag clicked
forever
    wait (2) seconds
    next backdrop
end
```

Click the flag. The music plays, and every 2 seconds the backdrop changes. After the last backdrop, `next backdrop` goes back around to the first one, so the light show never runs out.

## Step 4: The dancer

Click the **cat** in the sprite pane. This first script resets the cat, then makes it dance in place forever:

```scratch title="On the cat: reset and dance"
when green flag clicked
go to x: (0) y: (-60)
set rotation style [left-right v]
point in direction (90)
set size to (100) %
clear graphic effects
forever
    next costume
    wait (0.3) seconds
end
```

Click the flag. The cat stands in the middle and steps in place, switching costumes about three times a second.

:::tip
Make the dance match the music. Tap your foot to the song and change the `wait` number until the cat's steps land on the beat. Try `0.25` or `0.5`.
:::

## Step 5: Slide with the arrow keys

Add two new scripts to the cat, each with its own key hat:

```scratch title="On the cat: slide"
when [right arrow v] key pressed
point in direction (90)
change x by (15)

when [left arrow v] key pressed
point in direction (-90)
change x by (-15)
```

Press the arrows. The cat slides left and right and faces the way it's going. Because Step 4 set the rotation style to `left-right`, it flips to face left instead of turning upside down. And notice the dance keeps going while it slides: that's two scripts running at once on the same sprite.

## Step 6: Jump!

A jump is two loops: one going up and one coming down.

```scratch title="On the cat: jump"
when [space v] key pressed
repeat (10)
    change y by (10)
end
repeat (10)
    change y by (-10)
end
```

Press space. The cat rises 100 steps (10 times 10) and comes back down to exactly where it started. Both loops use the same numbers, so the landing spot always matches the takeoff spot.

Try pressing space again while the cat is in the air. Nothing happens! Scratch won't restart a key script that's still running, so the cat finishes its first jump before it can start another.

## Step 7: The pulse move

Make the cat swell and shrink when you press the up arrow:

```scratch title="On the cat: pulse"
when [up arrow v] key pressed
repeat (5)
    change size by (10)
end
repeat (5)
    change size by (-10)
end
```

The cat grows from 100% to 150% and back again in a quick pulse. It's the same pattern as the jump: do something, then undo it the same number of times.

## Step 8: Click to sing

Last script. When you click the cat, it should meow *while* the dance keeps going:

```scratch title="On the cat: sing"
when this sprite clicked
change [color v] effect by (25)
start sound (Meow v)
say [Meow!] for (1) seconds
```

Click the cat. It shifts to a new color, meows, and says "Meow!" for a second, and the music and dancing never stop. We chose `start sound` here because the meow and the bubble should happen at the same moment.

## Test the whole party

Click the green flag and try everything:

-   Music plays without stopping, and the backdrop changes every 2 seconds.
-   The cat dances in place.
-   Left and right slide it, space makes it jump, and up arrow makes it pulse.
-   Clicking it changes its color and makes it meow.
-   The stop sign stops everything.

Now click the green flag again. The cat should go back to the middle with its normal color and size. If it doesn't, check the reset blocks at the top of Step 4.

### Common bugs

-   **No music, or the song isn't in the dropdown.** You added the sound to the cat instead of the Stage. Each sprite and the Stage have their own sounds. Add the song to the Stage too, or move the music script onto the cat.
-   **The cat lands lower after each jump.** The two jump loops don't match. Both should repeat 10 times by 10.
-   **The cat gets bigger every time it pulses.** The two pulse loops don't match. Check the counts and the numbers.
-   **Pressing space scrolls the web page.** Click on the stage once so Scratch gets the key presses.

:::quiz Decisions in this build
Q: Why does the music loop use `play sound until done` instead of `start sound`?
- [x] So the loop waits for the song to finish before starting it again
  "Until done" holds the loop on each pass, so the song plays all the way through, then repeats.
- [ ] Because `start sound` only works on sprites, not the Stage
  Both blocks work on the Stage. The difference is whether the loop waits.
- [ ] So the music is louder
  Neither block changes the volume. That's `set volume to (100) %`.

Q: Your jump goes up with `repeat (10)` and `change y by (10)`, but comes down with `repeat (8)` and `change y by (-10)`. What happens after three jumps?
- [ ] Nothing changes, the cat always lands at the start
  It only lands at the start if the up and down trips are the same size.
- [x] The cat is 60 steps higher than where it started
  Each jump goes up 100 and down only 80, so it ends 20 higher. Three jumps leaves it 60 steps up.
- [ ] The cat falls through the floor
  It comes down less than it went up, so it drifts upward, not downward.

Q: You click the cat while it's dancing. Why doesn't the dance stop?
- [ ] Because the click script and the dance are in the same script
  They're separate scripts, each with its own hat block.
- [x] Because the click starts its own script, and the dance loop keeps running alongside it
  Scratch runs each script on its own, so starting one doesn't stop another.
- [ ] Because `start sound` pauses the dance until the meow ends
  `start sound` never pauses anything. That's why we picked it.
:::

:::takeaways
- The Stage can have scripts and sounds too. Put things that belong to the whole scene, like music, there.
- Several `forever` loops can run at once, even on the same sprite.
- Loop background music with `play sound until done`, so each pass waits for the song to end.
- "Do it, then undo it" moves like jumps and pulses need two loops that match exactly.
- A key script that's still running won't restart, so a jump always finishes before the next one.
:::

## Make it yours

-   Add a second dancer from the library, with its own dance loop and its own keys, like `w`, `a`, and `d`.
-   Use `when backdrop switches to [Spotlight v]` on the cat to do a special move whenever the spotlight comes on.
-   Add a key that runs `stop all sounds` for a dramatic pause. What happens to the music loop after it?

Save your project as `week2-dance-party.sb3`, or click **File > Save now**.
